Privacy Notice

Last updated August 14, 2026

This notice covers training.gpen.com, the product training portal for authorized G Pen retail partners. It explains what we collect when you certify on a product, who receives it, and how to get it removed.

The short version. To put your name on a certificate and issue you a discount code, we need your name, your work email and the store you work at. That information goes to G Pen and is stored in a training record. We do not use advertising trackers, we do not use cookies, and we do not sell or share your information with anyone for their own marketing.

Who this is for

This portal is for people aged 21 or over who work for an authorized G Pen retail partner. It is hardware and product education only — no cannabis, nicotine or e-liquid products are sold or shipped through this site. It is not directed at children, and we do not knowingly collect information from anyone under 21. Before starting a quiz you confirm you are 21 or older and authorized by your store; we record that confirmation and when you made it, because it is the basis on which the training was given to you.

What we collect

Information you give us

One form, shown before your first quiz:

  • Your name — printed on your certificate.
  • Your email address — identifies you across courses, and ties each discount code to you so it cannot be passed around.
  • Your store — so the completion is credited to your shop.
  • Your 21+ and authorization confirmation, and the time you gave it.

Information created as you train

  • Which courses you completed, your quiz score, and the date you passed.
  • A certificate ID for each certification.
  • The discount codes issued to you, and whether each has been redeemed on gpen.com.

Information stored on your own device

Your progress lives in your browser's local storage — not in cookies. That includes your enrollment details, your completed courses and certificates, your language choice, any discount codes already issued to you, and any quiz you are partway through. It stays on that device until you clear it. "Reset my progress" in the site footer erases all of it, including the saved codes.

Technical information

When a discount code is issued, our code-issuing service briefly counts requests by IP address to stop the codes being harvested in bulk. Those counters hold the IP address for roughly 48 hours and are not linked to your training record. Our hosting providers also keep standard server logs.

Your rights

You can ask us to:

  • tell you what we hold about you, and give you a copy;
  • correct anything wrong — a misspelled name on a certificate, the wrong store;
  • delete your training record;
  • stop using your information, or object to a particular use.

Email pr@grencoscience.com and say what you want done. We will need enough detail to find your record — normally the email address you trained with. We will respond within the time your local law allows.

Deleting your training record also gives up the certifications recorded in it. Any discount code already issued to you will still work until it expires, because the code exists in the store independently of your training record; tell us if you want those cancelled as well.

If you are in the UK or EEA you may complain to your data protection authority. If you are in California you have rights of access, correction, deletion and portability, and the right not to be discriminated against for using them — note that we do not sell or share personal information as those terms are defined there.

Security

Access to the training record is limited to the staff who run the program. The credentials that issue discount codes are held only on the server and are never sent to your browser. No system is perfectly secure, but we do not collect payment details, government identifiers or any special category information through this portal.

Changes

If we change this notice we will update the date at the top. If a change materially affects how we use information already collected, we will make that clear rather than relying on the date alone.
